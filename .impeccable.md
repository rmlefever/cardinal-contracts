## Design Context

### Users
Clinic administration and admissions staff use this while working inside patient records. Their job is to send the right contract quickly, track whether it has been signed, and recover the final signed PDF without administrative drag.

### Brand Personality
Calm, exact, trustworthy. The product should feel like a clinical operations tool: restrained, legible, and dependable rather than promotional.

### Aesthetic Direction
Light, paper-led interface with quiet institutional colors, compact controls, and strong scanability. Avoid glossy SaaS marketing patterns, oversized heroes, decorative gradients, and playful styling.

### Design Principles
Prioritize speed for repeated admin work. Keep the patient and payer context visible. Make contract status unmistakable. Prefer plain language over clever copy. Treat audit trails and document handling as first-class clinical operations.
